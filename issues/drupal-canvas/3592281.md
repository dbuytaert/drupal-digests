## Summary

In the Canvas editor, switching from a long page to a short page through the Pages panel could leave the top of the short page scrolled out of view. In the reported case it sat about 289px above the pane instead of about 50px below it. The preview now starts at its intended position after this switch.

## Impact

No upgrade or configuration changes required.

## Technical details

The fix is one CSS property, `overflow-anchor: none`, added to `.editorPane` (the scrollable pane that holds the preview). The editor sets this pane's scroll position itself. The issue's explanation is that the browser's scroll anchoring moved the scroll position when the preview shifted while the frame was catching up with a change in its size. Turning anchoring off stops the browser from adjusting the position on its own. The issue also reports a smaller, intermittent offset (about 58px instead of 50px) when loading a short page. It says a shared cause for the two symptoms was not confirmed, and the diff does not address that offset specifically.
