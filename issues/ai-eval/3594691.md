## Summary

In AI Eval trace review, anyone with only the Operate permission could label traces and promote them into datasets, bypassing the Annotate permission that run results already required. Operators can now view traces and existing labels but not change them. Labeling needs Annotate AI Eval results or Validate AI Eval judges. Promotion needs Annotate AI Eval results. Administrators keep access to everything. Roles that label or promote traces today need the annotate permission added, and this is noted under Upgrading in the changelog.

## Impact

Affects site owners.

- **Permission change:** Labeling traces now needs `annotate ai eval results`, `validate ai eval judges` or `administer ai eval`; promoting traces into datasets needs `annotate ai eval results` or `administer ai eval`. `operate ai eval` alone only views traces.

## Technical details

The `_permission` requirements changed on four POST routes. `ai_eval.review.label` and `ai_eval.trace_review.group_label` now use `annotate ai eval results+validate ai eval judges+administer ai eval`. `ai_eval.promote.trace` and `ai_eval.promote.bulk` use `annotate ai eval results+administer ai eval`. Validators are allowed to label so the gold-label and blind re-label screens work without Operate. Judge validators still cannot promote.

The UI follows route access instead of hardcoding it. `ReviewController`, `TraceReviewController` and `GoldLabelController` call `Url::access()` on the label, promote and bulk routes. Promote forms, bulk promote and the group verdict form are omitted when the route is forbidden, and `TraceReviewFragment::card()` and `groupVerdictBar()` render a read-only label or verdict instead. Read-only visitors see a notice naming the missing permissions. `GoldLabelController` no longer checks `operate ai eval` or `administer ai eval` directly for `$showActions`. The trace review group response adds the `user.permissions` cache context.

Permission descriptions in `ai_eval.permissions.yml` and the docs were updated to match. Added `TraceLabelPermissionsTest` covers all four roles against all four endpoints. Existing functional tests were updated to grant `annotate ai eval results`.

## Upgrade

Grant `annotate ai eval results` at `/admin/people/permissions` to every role that currently labels traces or promotes them into datasets. Roles that only label traces for judge validation can hold `validate ai eval judges` instead. Roles with only `operate ai eval` can still view traces but cannot change labels.
