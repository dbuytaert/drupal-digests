# #3594794: Add a judge for every criterion of a review rule

- **Project:** Drupal AI
- **Type:** Normal feature request
- **Status:** Fixed
- **Discussion:** [#3594794](https://git.drupalcode.org/project/ai_eval/-/work_items/3594794) · 1 contributor · 2 comments

## Summary

The AI Eval content review judge now covers every criterion of every AI Content Review rule, not just the one at the site-wide `criterion_index`. A rule with three criteria such as Tone & voice, Inclusive language and Grammar now has three judges. Existing judges keep their ids, so their scores and validations stay attached to the same criterion. The drush scoring command can target one criterion, and importing a fixture refuses to continue when the criterion at its recorded position has since changed label.

## Impact

Affects module maintainers.

- **New API:** Optional trailing criterion position argument on `CriterionRunner::reviewAsJudge()`, `judgeModel()`, `editorsModel()`, `review()`, `criterion()` and `ReviewRuleJudges::judgeId()`; new `content_review_criterion_at__<rule>__<index>` judge ids; new `--criterion` drush option on `ai-eval-cr:score-items`.

## Technical details

`ReviewRuleJudges::graderInfoAlter()` now loops over every criterion of each `review_rule`, plus the `criterion_index` from settings even if the rule has no criterion there. The criterion at that position keeps the existing id (`content_review_criterion`, or `content_review_criterion__<rule>` for other rules). All other positions get `content_review_criterion_at__<rule>__<index>`, counting from 0. This prefix is disjoint from the older ids, so a rule id containing `criterion_1` or `__` cannot collide with an existing judge. If another module already declares an id, that judge is skipped and a warning is logged. Ids follow positions, as AI Content Review's own `{rule}__{index}` names do, so reordering a rule's criteria moves its judges. Judge definitions carry `criterion_index` and `criterion_label`, and the label reads "Content review: <rule> / <criterion>".

`CriterionRunner::reviewAsJudge()`, `judgeModel()`, `editorsModel()`, `review()` and `criterion()`, and `ReviewRuleJudges::judgeId()`, take an optional trailing criterion position (`judgeId()` also takes the default index). `drush ai-eval-cr:score-items` has a new `--criterion` option (a position from 0, defaulting to the settings value) and writes `criterion_index` and `criterion_label` into the fixture meta. `import-run` rebuilds the judge id from that meta and errors with a request to give `--grader` when the label now at that position differs from the recorded one. The CHANGELOG notes it cannot distinguish criteria that share a label.

## Upgrade

Callers of the listed `CriterionRunner` methods and `ReviewRuleJudges::judgeId()` need no change: the new criterion position argument is optional and trailing. To score a non-default criterion from drush:

```
drush ai-eval-cr:score-items --items=items.yaml --criterion=2 --out=/tmp/out.yaml
```

Fixtures scored before this change have no `criterion_index` and import against the judge for the `criterion_index` in settings.
